# The Adventure of Kim

A 2D educational game that teaches elementary students decimals and fractions. Built with Godot 4.7 and GDScript.

This project is a rebuild and enhancement of my original thesis game from 2021, an Android game made in Godot 3.2. I rebuilt it from scratch with the same learning goals, plus new art, sound, lessons and controls, so it now runs on the Web, Windows and Android.

## About the game

Kim is a student at Masigasig Academy who thinks math is too hard. When his best friend Vince convinces him to join the Math Club, Kim trains through three stages to master decimals and fractions.

| Stage | Topic | Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Elite | Decimals | Collect 10 books while avoiding rabbits |
| 2 · Master | Fractions | Collect 15 pencils while avoiding rabbits and bats |
| 3 · Grand Master | Fractions and decimals | Collect books and pencils, then type every answer yourself |

## Features

- **Lessons from Vince:** each stage opens with worked examples and a practice question.
- **Learn as you explore:** every book and pencil Kim collects teaches a math fact.
- **Quizzes:** 20 questions per stage. Get at least 10 right on the first try to pass.
- **Life savers:** Kim has 3. Enemies and wrong answers cost one, and a wrong answer sends Kim to find another fact before trying again.
- **Plays anywhere:** keyboard, on-screen joystick or touch arrows.
- **Story and progress:** story scenes, saved progress and a completion certificate.
